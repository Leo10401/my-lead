What it does

Six real checks, not a black box

Heuristic, not a full audit — built to triage hundreds of leads fast, not replace the review you'd do before an actual pitch.

No linked website

Site unreachable

No mobile viewport

Over 75% of local search happens on phones. This alone kills conversions.

03
